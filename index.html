Hello and welcome

This archive only includes Western Saudi Arabia due to space limitations.

GUIDE:

  • Drag the map or use the mouse wheel / pinch to pan and zoom.
  • Scrub the slider or tap , / . (or arrow keys) to move through available snapshots.
  • Jump to key regions with the Mecca and Medina buttons.
  • Press H or use “Toggle HUD” to hide the interface for clean screenshots.
  • Press F to show or hide the draggable export frame; reposition it to capture a crop.
  • Open the Export menu to save 4K composites of Mecca, Medina, or the selected frame.
  • Use the Theme toggle to switch between dark and light backgrounds.

You can close this guide with the × button or by clicking the shaded backdrop.
